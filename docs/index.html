DBZ TCG Deck Helper

Build a Dragon Ball Z TCG deck (PanZ era, Sets 1–7) with a free chat AI such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. This page has no AI of its own. It gives your AI the real card info, then checks the deck it builds for rule mistakes.

1 Pick your Main Personality

The character your deck is built around.

2 Pick the styles to compare

Styles are the colours of cards. Pick 2 or 3 that you want your AI to weigh up (not sure? pick a few). Fewer styles make a smaller file, and free chats can only take so much.

3 Give the card info to your AI

Finish steps 1 and 2 first.

  1. Open a new chat with your AI.
  2. Paste the card info with the button below (or download it as a file and attach it to the chat).
  3. Then send this message:

      

      
If your AI says the text is too long, go back to step 2 and pick fewer styles. Your AI will then read the card info and suggest styles and a deck. When it gives you a final list, come back here for step 4.

    

  


  

    
4 Check the deck your AI built

    
Paste its final deck list here. This checks the rules (60 cards, copy limits, character-only cards and so on) and gives you the deck code to type into the deck builder app.
