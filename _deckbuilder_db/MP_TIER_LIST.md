# Main Personality tier list, Sets 1-7 (PanZ), ranked on printed abilities only

Generated by `scripts/mp_tier_list.py` (the scores live in that file; edit them there and re-run). Card text is `text_current` when present, otherwise `text_as_printed`.

## How to read this
- **Scope:** every Main Personality printed in Sets 1-7, grouped by character (Goku Premiere + Evolution + Awakening = "Goku"). Set 8+ (FanZ) MPs, the OP promos and Dende (OP3 only) are out of scope. 30 characters, 39 level-stacks.
- **Ranked on abilities only.** Power stages, PUR and rarity are ignored. The one place that bites: a few MPs have tiny printed power (Hercule, Captain Ginyu's Frog, Dr. Wheelo), so their real strength depends on stats this list does not look at.
- **Scoring:** each level gets a hand-assigned 1-10 score from its printed text. 10 = changes the game each turn; 7-8 = clearly strong (big flat life-card bonus, unstoppable or unpreventable, repeatable defense or tutor); 5-6 = solid but conditional or low-impact; 1-4 = filler, a drawback or needs outside support. Scores are my judgement, not a simulation, so treat gaps under about 0.5 as ties.
- **Multiple stacks:** the rules let you mix printings of the same name and alignment level by level, so a character's score is the **mean of the best available printing at each level**. A character with several stacks (Goku, Trunks, Piccolo) gets credit for the best of them, but not for more than one pick per level. Each stack's own mean is also shown so you can see which set carried the character. Hero and Villain Vegeta cannot mix, so his score is his better alignment.
- **Tiers:** S = 7.5 or more, A = 6.75-7.49, B = 6.0-6.74, C = 5.25-5.99, D = under 5.25.
- **Not modelled:** deck-building support (how many cards exist for an archetype), Mastery interactions, matchups, and the Frozen list. Conditional cards (Android 17, Cell Jr., Cell Set 7) are scored as if their support exists but are marked down for needing it.

**What this shows (judgement, small sample, hand-scored):**
- Power creep exists but is modest and uneven: mean stack score goes 6.50 (Set 1) to 6.78 (Set 7), with dips at Set 2 and Set 4 and no steady climb. Set 1 was not weak; its text already had 4-7 life-card attacks (Piccolo, Krillin, Captain Ginyu).
- The **top** moved up the most: the two best stacks in the game are Set 7 Deck Reveal Vegeta (Hero) and Goku, built on flat bonuses to every attack (+2 to +3 life cards) plus 'cannot be stopped'; the catch is that Vegeta's bonuses only apply to non-Styled attacks and Goku's are spread across draw/tutor effects. Nothing printed before Set 5 scores above 7.75.
- The **floor** rose too: every Set 7 stack scores 6.0 or more, while Sets 1, 2 and 4 contain 4.75-5.5 stacks (Trunks, Nappa, Raditz, Nail, Vegeta Hero Set 4, Frieza, the Android 17 starter).
- Same-character comparisons are mixed, not one-way: Goku, Trunks and Hero Vegeta get clearly better by Set 7 (Hero Vegeta +3.5 from Set 4, Trunks +2.25 from Set 1), but Piccolo and Gohan were already at their best in Set 1 and later stacks are weaker.
- Where it shows most in the text: life-card damage bonuses and unstoppable text become common in Set 7 (see the counts above), and the Level 4 average rises from 6.4 to 7.4.

## Notes and caveats
- **Hercule\*:** Level 1 literally says his attacks deal +100 stages of damage. If that works as printed it is a one-hit threat, but whether it interacts with his own 'cannot be modified by your effects' line, and what happens to stages lost past 0 power, is not settled in the notes here (it is **not** verified). He is scored 8 on that level and 6-7 elsewhere; if the literal reading is wrong he drops about half a tier.
- **Frieza Golden (Set 3 promo)** damage is `AT life cards`, which depends on the Attack Table result and is scored 7 rather than higher for that reason.
- **Android 16, Garlic Jr. Level 4** have printed drawbacks / symmetric effects; they are scored for the whole text, not the upside.
- **Krillin Level 3** (9 minus cards in opp's hand) is strong only if the opponent's hand is small; the notes say the defender draws 3 on entering combat, so it needs hand disruption.
- Scores for Set 7 Deck Reveal stacks (Goku, Vegeta, Trunks) assume the deck can use the Styled / non-Styled split those cards care about.
