## 2. Visual re-read (random 5% sample)

A seeded random sample of 159 cards (5% of every set/folder, at least one each; ids in `work/crd/qa_sample.json`) was re-read from the original images and compared with `cards.json` (name, subtitle, type, number, 11 stages and PUR for personalities, alignment, Traits, Endurance, every sentence of text and icon).
**Errors found: 0 of 159 cards (0.0%).** With zero errors in 159 cards the 95% upper bound on the card-level error rate is about 1.9% (rule of three). The sample had 45 personalities (37 MP, 8 Ally; every stage and PUR matched), 36 Physical and 26 Energy Combat cards, 21 Events, 14 Setups, 13 Drills, 2 Masteries and 2 Dragon Balls, from PanZ (83) and FanZ (76), in every collection.
Caveats: the comparison was by eye on review sheets, so a one-word difference in a long text could still be missed; errors found and fixed *during* Phase 2 (for example wrong inline Trait icons, a Mastery number, a title with a stray '!') are not counted here.

## 8. Known limits

- `text_current` is derived from the CRD erratum text; where a CRD 'This Card Gains' line goes on the corrected card is not stated.
- Fourteen S07 'never officially released' promos have most text hidden by an overlay; their text is copied from the reprinted card and flagged.
- Two S04 promos (P9, P10) are 'Image N/A' placeholders: only the CRD-based identity is known.
- The black warning-triangle icon (`[Warning]`, Set 16+) has no explanation on the cards or in the CRD.
- Trait icons were identified by zooming each inline icon, but a few small icons in the early FanZ sets were read by colour.
